Welcome to Mamma Rosa

At Mamma Rosa, pizza is more than food — it is memory, tradition, and family.

Our story begins in a small village where life was simple, people worked hard, and nothing was ever wasted. We come from humble rural roots, where the land gave us everything and respect for food was part of everyday life.

My grandfather worked the fields from sunrise to sunset, growing vegetables, olives, tomatoes, and everything the seasons could offer. My other grandfather repaired and handmade shoes for the hardworking people of the village, and many times they would pay not with money, but with their own produce: fresh eggs, homemade cheese, wine, flour, fresh vegetables from their gardens.


It was a different world, built on honesty, community and gratitude.

As a child, I loved following my grandfather into the fields. What many would call hard work felt like an adventure to me. Those days became some of the happiest memories of my life — the smell of fresh earth, homemade bread, tomatoes picked under the summer sun, and long family meals around the table.

“Looking back now, I feel truly blessed to have lived those experiences, because they shaped the person I am today.”

The soul of Mamma Rosa also comes from my mother, Rosa. Since she was a little girl, cooking was part of her life. She learned recipes, techniques, and traditions passed down through generations with love, patience, and passion.

From her, I learned that good food takes time, care, and respect for ingredients.

Today, our pizzeria brings those values to the table every single day. We believe in simple, honest cooking made with quality ingredients, inspired by the traditions of true farm-to-table living.

Every pizza carries a piece of our family history — years of experience, sacrifice, passion and love for real food.

Mamma Rosa is not just a pizzeria.

It is a tribute to our family, to traditional Italian village life, and to all the people who taught us that even with little, you can create something beautiful when it is made with heart.
